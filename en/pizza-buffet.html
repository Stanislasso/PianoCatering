Restaurant quality, at your party too

Piano C is the catering of Piano A, the Apulian restaurant of Alessandro, Andrea and Mattia on Ravenstraat in Leuven. What we serve at your party comes from the same kitchen: the same dough, the same tomatoes, mozzarella and olive oil, the same recipes from Puglia.

So no separate catering menu, but the dishes that are on the table in the restaurant every evening. Curious? Piano A is simply open, you are always welcome.
