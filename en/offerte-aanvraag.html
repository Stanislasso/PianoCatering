    Frequently asked questions

    Good to know

    What is the minimum number of guests?

    Twenty. Below twenty guests we charge for twenty, because we come with the same crew and the same oven. There is no upper limit: up to 150 guests we work with one oven, beyond that we plan it together.

    What is included in the price?

    The dishes you choose, our team on site, the mobile wood-fired oven or kitchen, set-up and clean-up. The €200 set-up fee is spread over your guests, so the quote page shows you an honest price per person right away.

    Drinks, starters and desserts are chosen separately. Plates and glasses are arranged together, depending on your venue.

    Are prices with or without VAT?

    All prices on the site are excluding VAT. Food carries 12 % VAT, drinks 21 %. The quote and the invoice clearly state the amount including VAT.

    How far do you travel?

    Within 20 km driving distance of Piano A (Ravenstraat 38, Leuven) there is no transport fee. Beyond that we charge €1.35 per extra kilometre. The quote page calculates it automatically from your address.

    How do set-up and timing work?

    We arrive one and a half to two hours before the meal to set up. We need a flat spot of a few metres for the oven, preferably outdoors or under a roof, and a power socket nearby. The pizza buffet runs for two hours, pasta and the rest follow your programme. Afterwards we clean everything up.

    What about allergies and vegetarians?

    Vegetarian pizzas and pastas are always part of the offer. Mention allergies or diets in your request and we adjust the menu. Because we work with wheat flour in one oven, we cannot guarantee a coeliac-safe meal; we look for a solution together.

    Can we really taste it first?

    Yes, and we recommend it. Book a table at Piano A, Ravenstraat 38 in Leuven, and eat what you would like to serve at your party. What you get there is what your guests get. Mention when booking that you are coming to taste for an event.

    How is this different from regular catering?

    A classic caterer has a separate catering menu, with products and recipes that differ from a restaurant. We do not. The dough rises in the kitchen of Piano A, the tomatoes, mozzarella and olive oil come from the same stock as the restaurant, and the same cooks bake at your party. Our food cost is higher because of it, and you can taste it.

    How and when do we pay?

    No deposit. After the party you receive an invoice, payable within 14 days. You confirm the final number of guests two weeks in advance. The full rules are in our terms and conditions.
